# Service Level Agreement and Priority Levels

Document owner: Service Delivery
Last reviewed: January 2026
Applies to: all managed-service contracts

## How priority is assigned

Priority is assigned by the service desk at the point the ticket is
raised, based on the impact of the fault and whether the affected area
is occupied. Priority is not chosen by the person reporting the fault,
although their assessment is recorded on the ticket.

Priority can be changed after the ticket is raised. Any change must be
recorded on the ticket with a reason, and raising a priority to P1
requires the agreement of the duty Site Manager.

## P1 - Critical

A fault that presents an immediate risk to health and safety, makes a
building unusable, or stops a client's core business operation. Examples
include total power loss, a fire alarm fault, a lift entrapment, or a
major water leak.

Response time: 1 hour, at any hour of the day or night.
Target resolution: 4 hours.

## P2 - High

A fault that significantly affects the use of a building or an important
area within it, but where the building remains usable. Examples include
loss of heating or cooling to an occupied floor, a failed access control
door on a main entrance, or a single lift out of service in a building
with more than one.

Response time: 4 hours within the standard service window.
Target resolution: 1 working day.

## P3 - Medium

A fault that causes inconvenience but does not materially affect the use
of the building. Examples include a single failed light fitting in a
common area, a slow-draining sink, or a noisy fan unit.

Response time: 1 working day.
Target resolution: 5 working days.

## P4 - Low

Planned work, cosmetic issues, and requests with no fault attached, such
as moving furniture or fitting additional signage.

Response time: 3 working days.
Target resolution: 20 working days, or by an agreed scheduled date.

## Clock stops

The resolution clock is paused when Northwind is waiting on the client
for access, for a decision, or for approval of costs above the agreed
threshold. The pause must be recorded on the ticket with the reason and
the time it started.

The clock is not paused while waiting for a Northwind supplier. Supplier
delays remain Northwind's responsibility under all managed-service
contracts.
