# Site Access and Contractor Control

Document owner: Health and Safety
Last reviewed: February 2026
Applies to: all Northwind Facilities sites

## Access badges

All Northwind staff and long-term contractors hold a photographic access
badge issued by the site they are primarily based at. Badges are not
transferable between people and must be worn visibly at all times inside
the building.

A lost badge must be reported to the service desk the same day. The old
badge is deactivated immediately and a replacement is issued within two
working days. A charge of 25 EUR applies to the second and each
subsequent replacement in a rolling twelve-month period.

## Visiting contractors

Contractors attending for a single job sign in at reception and are
issued a temporary badge valid for that day only. They must be escorted
in plant rooms, on roofs, and in any area marked as restricted.

The Northwind engineer who raised the job is responsible for the
contractor while they are on site, including their sign-out. A
contractor who has not signed out by the end of the working day is
reported to the duty Site Manager.

## Permits to work

A permit to work is required for hot works, work at height above two
metres, confined space entry, and any work on live electrical systems.

Permits are issued by the duty Site Manager and are valid for one shift
only. A permit cannot be extended; if the work overruns, a new permit is
issued.

Work covered by a permit may not begin until the permit is physically
present at the work location.

## Keys and restricted areas

Plant room keys are held at reception and signed out against a named
individual. Keys must be returned the same day.

Comms rooms and client server rooms are not accessible to Northwind
staff without the client's own authorisation, arranged in advance
through the Regional Facilities Manager. This applies even during a P1
fault.

## Working alone

Lone working is permitted for routine inspection and light maintenance
during the standard service window.

Lone working is not permitted for work at height, work on live
electrical systems, confined space entry, or any after-hours attendance
at an unoccupied building. Night callouts are attended by two people as
a minimum.
