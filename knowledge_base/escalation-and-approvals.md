# Escalation and Approval Thresholds

Document owner: Facilities Operations
Last reviewed: March 2026
Applies to: all Northwind Facilities staff

## Spend approval thresholds

Up to 500 EUR: approved by the duty Site Manager.

500 to 2,500 EUR: approved by the Regional Facilities Manager.

2,500 to 10,000 EUR: approved by the Head of Facilities Operations.

Above 10,000 EUR: approved by the Finance Director, and requires a
written quotation from at least two approved suppliers.

These thresholds apply to the total cost of the work including labour,
parts and callout charges. Work may not be split into smaller orders to
stay below a threshold.

## Emergency spend

Where a fault presents an immediate risk to health and safety, the duty
Site Manager may authorise spend up to 5,000 EUR without further
approval. The Head of Facilities Operations must be informed by 09:00
the following working day and the ticket must record what was authorised
and why.

This emergency provision applies only to P1 faults.

## Escalating a ticket

A ticket is escalated when it is at risk of breaching its resolution
target, when the client has expressed dissatisfaction, or when the cost
has risen above the approval already obtained.

First escalation is to the duty Site Manager. Second escalation is to
the Regional Facilities Manager. Third escalation is to the Head of
Facilities Operations, who owns the relationship with the client's
nominated contact.

Escalations are recorded on the ticket. An escalation that is not
recorded is treated as not having happened.

## Client complaints

Any complaint from a client contact is logged as a P3 ticket against the
site, regardless of the subject, and assigned to the Regional Facilities
Manager within one working day.

Complaints concerning safety are logged as P1 and are notified to the
Head of Facilities Operations immediately.

## Out-of-hours escalation contacts

The duty Site Manager rota is published weekly and is available from the
service desk. Outside the standard service window, the service desk
holds the current duty numbers for all four sites.

The Regional Facilities Manager is contactable out of hours for night
callouts and P1 escalations only.
