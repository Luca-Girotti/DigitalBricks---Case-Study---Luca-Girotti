# After-Hours Callout Policy

Document owner: Facilities Operations
Last reviewed: March 2026
Applies to: all Northwind Facilities sites

## What counts as an after-hours callout

An after-hours callout is any attendance at a client site outside the
standard service window of 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, excluding
public holidays in the country where the site is located.

Attendance between 18:00 and 22:00 on a weekday is classed as an evening
callout. Attendance between 22:00 and 08:00, or at any time on a Saturday,
Sunday or public holiday, is classed as a night callout.

## Requesting a callout

Callouts are requested through the service desk, never directly with the
engineer. The requester must provide the site, the nature of the fault,
and whether the building is occupied.

If the building is occupied and the fault affects life safety systems,
the service desk raises the callout immediately and seeks approval
afterwards. In all other cases approval must be obtained before the
engineer is dispatched.

## Who approves an after-hours callout

Evening callouts are approved by the duty Site Manager for the site in
question.

Night callouts, and any callout expected to exceed four hours, require
approval from the Regional Facilities Manager. If the Regional Facilities
Manager cannot be reached within thirty minutes, the duty Site Manager
may approve the callout and must notify the Regional Facilities Manager
by 09:00 the following working day.

Callouts to a site where Northwind holds a reactive-only contract require
approval from the client's own nominated contact in addition to the
Northwind approver.

## Callout rates

Evening callouts are charged at 1.5 times the standard hourly rate, with
a minimum of two hours.

Night, weekend and public holiday callouts are charged at 2.0 times the
standard hourly rate, with a minimum of four hours.

Travel time to and from site is charged at the standard hourly rate
regardless of the hour, and is not subject to the minimum.

## Recording a callout

Every callout must have a ticket raised against it before the engineer
leaves site, even where the callout was verbally approved. The ticket
records the approver's name, the time of approval, and the time the
engineer arrived on site.

Callouts without a ticket cannot be invoiced to the client and are
absorbed by Northwind.
