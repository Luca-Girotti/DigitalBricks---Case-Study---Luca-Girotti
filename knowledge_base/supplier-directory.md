# Approved Supplier Directory

Document owner: Procurement
Last reviewed: February 2026
Applies to: Antwerp, Amsterdam, Rotterdam and Brussels sites

## How to use this directory

Only suppliers listed in this directory may be used without a separate
procurement approval. Using an unlisted supplier requires sign-off from
Procurement before the order is placed, even for small amounts.

Where a site has both a primary and a secondary supplier, the secondary
is used only when the primary cannot meet the required response time.
The reason for using the secondary must be noted on the ticket.

## HVAC and ventilation

Antwerp: HVAC filters and consumables are supplied by Verhoeven Klimaat
BV. They are the primary supplier for all filter changes, belts and
bearings at the Antwerp site. Standard lead time is two working days.
The secondary supplier for Antwerp is Lucht & Co.

Amsterdam and Rotterdam: filters and consumables are supplied by
Klimaatpartners Nederland. Lead time is one working day for stocked
items.

Brussels: filters are supplied by Verhoeven Klimaat BV under the same
terms as Antwerp.

Major HVAC plant repairs at all four sites go to Thermex Service Group,
regardless of which supplier provides the consumables.

## Lifts and escalators

All four sites use Otis Benelux under a single group maintenance
contract. Entrapments are called through the Otis emergency line, not
through the Northwind service desk, and the service desk raises the
ticket retrospectively.

## Electrical

Antwerp and Brussels: Elektro Vanden Berghe.
Amsterdam and Rotterdam: Stroomwerk BV.

Both suppliers hold a four-hour emergency response commitment for P1
faults.

## Cleaning and waste

All sites use Helder Facility Services for daily cleaning. Specialist
cleaning, including post-incident and high-level cleaning, goes to
PurePoint Specialist Cleaning.
